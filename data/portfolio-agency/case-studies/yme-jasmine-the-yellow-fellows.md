# YME - Jasmine & The Yellow Fellows


*Producer: Musicater Indonesia*

*Distributed by Demajors*

*YME* (Yang Maha Esa) is a single performed by Jasmine together with *Jasmine and the Yellow Fellows*. The lyrics were created by Nabilla Jasmine, inspired by themes from Surah *Ar-Rahman* and *Al-Baqarah*, while the music was composed by Arini Kumara.

![](/images/uploads/images.jpeg "Nabilla Jasmine and The Yellow Fellows – Musicater Indonesia ")

The song reflects on God’s blessings and the importance of gratitude, embracing faith in a way that ultimately leads everything toward goodness.


This single was produced by Musicater Indonesia, a collaborative music project I am part of together with Arini Kumara and Alexandro Subianto. In this project, I contributed on keyboard and electric piano, supporting the song’s harmonic texture and overall atmosphere.
