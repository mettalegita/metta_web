**Saturday, 27 June 2026** marked a memorable evening as I welcomed audiences into my home for **The Living Room Series – Volume 3: *In Full Bloom*** in Phnom Penh.

![](/images/uploads/att.cin_vnlkjdhhsore7rlvrhfjozc1ikk4c0veftwyzu0.jpg "The Living Room Series Concert Vol. 3 ")

Inspired by one of my abstract rose paintings, *In Full Bloom* celebrated the beauty of growth, creativity, and the many colors of life. My vision was to create more than a concert—I wanted to offer an intimate artistic experience where music, visual art, and human connection could flourish together.

![](/images/uploads/5bcf904b-0dba-4fd4-a034-b8f840460e88-2.png)

For this special occasion, my living room was transformed into a rose-inspired garden, filled with red roses, warm decorative lights, and carefully curated artwork. Every detail was thoughtfully prepared to create a welcoming atmosphere where guests could relax, listen deeply, and share an unforgettable evening of music.

![](/images/uploads/att.hysg7l9dopmqsvc-9n6ym2ztes8wfh3ykd0hwp4hk3g.jpg)

The program reflected many different facets of my musical journey. Alongside jazz standards, improvisations, and my own original compositions, I was delighted to perform several works from the classical repertoire, including Claude Debussy's *Clair de Lune*, Erik Satie's *Gymnopédie*, and *Fragmen* by Indonesian composer Jaya Suprana, which concluded the evening with a reflective and heartfelt atmosphere. Bringing these different musical worlds together felt both inspiring and deeply personal.

![]()

One of the highlights of the evening was the appearance of my special guests, **Colin Grafton** and **Keiko Kitamura**. Together, we performed *Parangtritis Paradise*, a composition we created during the Festival Musik Rumah project, Colin with his harmonica and Keiko with the Cambodian jaw harp called angkuoch. Sharing this music together once again made the evening even more meaningful.

![](/images/uploads/img_2714.jpg)

This concert also marked my first collaboration with Indonesian sound engineer **Deon Manunggal**, who is based in Phnom Penh. I am sincerely grateful for his professionalism, dedication, and support in helping bring this performance to life.

![](/images/uploads/img_2498.heic)

*In Full Bloom* was a concert I had anticipated for many months. Every aspect—from selecting the repertoire and rehearsing the music to designing the concert space—was prepared with great enthusiasm, joy, and care. Seeing the audience enjoy the music in such an intimate setting made every moment of preparation worthwhile.

![](/images/uploads/img_ba23d6788bfa-1.jpeg)

My heartfelt thanks go to everyone who attended, as well as to all the music lovers, supporters, patrons, friends, and family whose encouragement made this evening possible. Your presence, kindness, and belief in this concert series continue to inspire me to create meaningful musical experiences.

![](/images/uploads/img_2500.heic)

As the evening came to a close, I found myself already looking ahead to the next chapter of this musical journey. **The Living Room Series – Volume 4** will take place on **25–26 July 2026**, featuring Indonesian vocalist **Intan Andriana**, who has built an international career while living in Cambodia and recently released her newest album. Together, we will present **A Garden of Songs**, an intimate celebration of jazz, storytelling, and musical friendship. I look forward to welcoming everyone once again into my living room for another unforgettable evening of music.

![](/images/uploads/img_2505.heic)

Until then, thank you for being part of this journey, and I look forward to sharing more music with you very soon.

**— Metta Legita**
