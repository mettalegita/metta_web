# A Memorable Honor: Performing for the 60th Years Indonesia–Cambodia Friendship Cultural Celebration

On 13 February 2019, I had the profound honor of performing in Phnom Penh as part of the 60th anniversary celebration of diplomatic relations between Indonesia and Cambodia. The event, held at the iconic Chaktomouk Theater, was a beautiful expression of cultural exchange and friendship between the two nations.

## Details

Together with my chamber orchestra and Indonesian singer Intan Andriana

![](/images/uploads/img_9995.jpg "Performing for the 60th Years Indonesia–Cambodia Friendship Cultural Celebration")

I was privileged to present a program that reflected the spirit of collaboration and shared artistic heritage. The evening was especially meaningful as it brought together distinguished guests, including representatives from Indonesia such as Kinarya, led by Guruh Sukarno Putra.

![](/images/uploads/img_0646.jpg)

### Memorable Moments

One of the most unforgettable moments for me personally was performing in the presence of His Majesty the King of Cambodia, and having the honor to greet him. It was a deeply humbling experience that I will always cherish. 

![](/images/uploads/img_0651.jpg)

I would like to express my heartfelt gratitude to the Embassy of the Republic of Indonesia in Phnom Penh, especially H.E. Sudirman Haseng, and Ibu Santi as Cultural Diplomat, for their trust and support in making this performance possible.

This event was more than a performance—it was a celebration of friendship, culture, and connection. I am truly grateful to have been part of such a meaningful and historic occasion.
