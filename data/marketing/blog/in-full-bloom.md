🌹 IN FULL BLOOM 🌹

This special evening is inspired by one of my abstract rose paintings and the idea of blooming — embracing beauty, growth, creativity, and the many colors of life.

For this concert, I will transform my living room into a rose-inspired space filled with flowers, soft lighting, artwork, and an intimate atmosphere designed to bring music and visual art together.

The program will feature solo piano performances, jazz standards, improvisations, and original music in a warm and personal setting.
