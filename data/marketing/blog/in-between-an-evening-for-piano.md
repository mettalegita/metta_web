### **In Between — An Evening for Piano**

This is the first concert of The Living Room Series.



A close listening experience, held in a living room setting.



I will share a selection of my original works and repertoire, in a setting designed for close listening and presence. Alongside the music, I will also share a selection of my paintings.

### Details

**Date:** Saturday, 25 April 2026
**Time:** Evening
**Location:** Phnom Penh (private residence, details upon confirmation)



**Suggested contribution:** $5–10 (or more if you wish)
Light refreshments will be available



If you feel connected to this, you are warmly invited.

— Metta
