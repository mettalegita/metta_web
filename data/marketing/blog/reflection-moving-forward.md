# Reflection: Moving Forward

![](/images/uploads/chatgpt-image-jun-4-2026-09_59_42-am.png "our poster")

## As I prepare for The Living Room Series Vol. 2, I find myself reflecting on the journey that brought me here.

This evening's program brings together selected works by Chick Corea and original compositions, alongside the wonderful musicians Sharon Lui and Mariko Fukao. Corea's music has long been a source of inspiration for me, reminding me of the joy of exploration, playfulness, and connection.

![](/images/uploads/screen-shot-2026-06-04-at-10.19.04.png "the programs")

The painting used for this concert was created several years ago, around 2023—or perhaps even earlier. I honestly cannot remember exactly when. What I do remember is sharing it with friends, and Sharon being one of the first people to see it. At the time, I called it Secret Garden Concert. Looking at it now, it feels as though the painting had been quietly waiting for the right moment to become part of this series.

What makes this concert different is that, for the first time, I decided to announce it publicly on Facebook. As someone who is not naturally an event host, I found myself feeling a little awkward and uncertain. I wondered whether the concert would happen, whether people would come, and what others might think.

Fortunately, those thoughts were like clouds passing through the sky. They appeared, stayed for a while, and eventually moved on. The concert happened. People came. The room filled with music, conversation, and shared attention. By the end of the evening, it was a full house.

> Perhaps that is what Moving Forward means to me: not moving without doubt, but continuing despite it.

![](/images/uploads/att.fue6jobj7nto0-dfn1s1ibpl99r7tkdh7lexiiddayk.jpg)

I am deeply grateful to Sharon Lui and Mariko Fukao for bringing their artistry, generosity, and friendship to this concert. I am equally thankful to everyone who supported the event, attended, encouraged, and helped create such a warm atmosphere.

> Thank you for being part of this journey. What began as an idea, a painting, and a few uncertain steps became an evening of connection that I will always remember.
